### What is explicitly not claimed

- No winrate, expectancy or drawdown improvement is claimed for v2.1.
- The score symmetry fix removes a structural SHORT blocker; it does not prove
  that the resulting signals are profitable.
- `tm_modeFilterOn = true` intentionally reduces the number of signals. Any
  comparison against v2.0 must publish candidate/eligible/filled counts.
